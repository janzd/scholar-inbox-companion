# Chrome Web Store listing draft

## Name

Scholar Inbox Companion

## Short description

Save papers from arXiv, CVF, OpenReview and other paper pages or PDFs to your Scholar Inbox collections.

## Detailed description

Save the paper you are reading to your Scholar Inbox collections, directly from your browser.

Scholar Inbox Companion finds the paper's existing Scholar Inbox entry and lets you choose a collection without opening Scholar Inbox and searching for the paper again.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Sign in to your Scholar Inbox account in the same Chrome profile.
2. Open a paper page or PDF and click the Companion toolbar icon.
3. Check the matched paper, choose a collection, and save.

PAPER SOURCES

Works with arXiv, CVF Open Access, and OpenReview paper pages. It also reads scholarly metadata on other websites and can extract a title from supported public PDFs or a downloaded PDF you choose. If a match is ambiguous, choose the correct paper or edit the title and search again.

YOUR COLLECTIONS

Search your existing collections, see where a paper is already saved, and open its Scholar Inbox entry. Read-only collections are identified in the picker. Saving checks the result before showing a confirmed success.

YOUR APPEARANCE

Choose Light, Dark, or System appearance, with either blue accents or the warm Scholar Inbox color palette. Settings are remembered across popup openings.

BEFORE YOU START

A Scholar Inbox account and an existing sign-in are required. Papers must already be indexed in Scholar Inbox; the extension does not import new records. Create and manage your collections in Scholar Inbox. Some restricted pages, protected PDFs, and scanned documents may require a manual title search or may not be supported.

This is an unofficial companion and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Scholar Inbox.
