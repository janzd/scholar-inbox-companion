Digest notifications

Show a NEW badge on the extension when today’s digest is available. Opening that digest clears the badge.

Chrome will ask for notification permission. Click a notification to open the digest reader. Checks run while Chrome is running; sleep and system notification settings can delay alerts.

Slack notifications

Choose a channel webhook or a direct message from your Slack bot. Your workspace may require app approval.

Install your Slack app with chat:write and im:write bot permissions. Copy its Bot User OAuth Token from OAuth & Permissions. In Slack, copy your member ID from your profile’s menu. No message-reading permissions are needed.

Stored only in this Chrome profile, not synced or encrypted by the extension. Treat the URL as a secret. Leaving it blank keeps the saved webhook.

Bot tokens are also stored locally, not synced or encrypted by the extension. Leave the token blank to keep it. Switching delivery methods replaces the previous connection.

Uses the Scholar Inbox account signed in when enabled. Hourly checks must also be on. Future checks can post a generic digest notice and a Scholar Inbox link; no paper titles or abstracts are sent.

Discord notifications

Choose a channel webhook or a direct message from your Discord bot. Your server must allow webhooks or bot installation.

Create a bot in the Discord Developer Portal and install it in a server you share with it. Copy the token from its Bot page. Turn on Developer Mode in Discord, then right-click your profile and Copy User ID. Your DM privacy settings must allow the bot to message you. Use a bot token, not your personal account token.

Stored only in this Chrome profile, not synced or encrypted by the extension. Treat the URL as a secret. Leaving it blank keeps the saved webhook.

Bot tokens are also stored locally, not synced or encrypted by the extension. Leave the token blank to keep it. Switching delivery methods replaces the previous connection.

Uses the Scholar Inbox account signed in when enabled. Hourly checks must also be on. Future checks can post a generic digest notice and a Scholar Inbox link; no paper titles or abstracts are sent.
